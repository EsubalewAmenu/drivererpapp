DriverERPApp — marketing brief

Elevator pitch
A lightweight, schema-driven ERP frontend that lets teams build and adapt forms, modules and dashboards without code, using Google Drive for storage and collaboration.

Value proposition
- Configure forms, tables and dashboards in-app
- Store data in Drive for native sharing and backups
- Quick deployment with cross-platform Flutter client

Primary channels
- Dev & product community (GitHub, Product Hunt)
- LinkedIn Ads targeting operations managers and CTOs at SMBs
- Tech blogs and Slack communities for startups and field-service apps

Collateral & assets
- 90s explainer video showing schema editing -> form -> record flow
- One-page PDF: features + pricing + screenshots
- Demo workspace with sample company and module templates

Call to action
- Try a hosted demo or download the app and connect Drive to get started in under 5 minutes.
