DriverERPApp — non-technical summary for stakeholders

What it does
- Enables teams to build forms, tables and module dashboards without coding.
- Persists data using Google Drive, enabling familiar file access and sharing.

Who benefits
- Small and medium businesses needing customizable workflows
- Field teams collecting records (inspections, deliveries, inventory)
- Teams who want low-ops solutions without a full backend

How customers use it
1. Create workspace and company profile
2. Define schema (fields, tabs, dashboard cards)
3. Create module and start adding records via dynamic forms
4. Use Drive for backups and sharing, view dashboards for insights

Business models (suggested)
- Freemium: limited modules/workspaces; paid tiers for team seats and advanced features
- Hosted Pro: managed hosting, advanced analytics, SSO and custom integrations
- Professional services: schema templates, onboarding, migrations

Support & success
- Provide quickstart guides, sample templates and a demo workspace
- Offer premium onboarding and template customization for higher-tier customers

Success metrics
- Time-to-first-record (goal: < 10 minutes)
- Active modules per workspace
- Conversion rate from freemium to paid within 30 days
